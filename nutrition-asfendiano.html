Asfendiano

LOT A2201

Photo of the Asfendiano bottle & label — coming soon

This page currently shows our production and technical specifications. Full lab-verified nutritional information (calories, sugars, sulfites, etc.) is pending lab results and will be added here once available.
Composition60% Merlot, 30% Syrah, 10% Black Lady
CertificationsCertified Natural Wine
Vines17-year-old vines on limestone, ~300m elevation. Unwatered grapes, minimum intervention.
Yield400kg / 1,000m²
Harvest periodMid-August (10th–20th) for Syrah; early September (1st–5th) for Cabernet Sauvignon.
Vinification methodBoiled must. No added sugar. No sulphides.
Maturation & ageing6 months in stainless steel tanks, then 6 months in bottle with natural ageing corks. Traditionally drunk at Christmas, or chilled in summer.
Ageing potentialUp to 5 years maximum
Alcohol13%

Different LOT numbers denote different bottling batches. The year of harvest is written on the front label. The first two digits of the LOT number denote the year of bottling (usually one to two years after harvest); the last two digits denote the batch number, and usually make no difference to taste — different batches can come from the same barrel.
