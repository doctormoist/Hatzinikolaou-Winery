04

Asfendiano

€24

Sweet dessert wine, no added sugar · 13% ABV

Made from 100% grapes, with no added sugar or sweetener. Its sweetness comes from boiling part of the must, which we call “psimma.” It tastes like caramel, and can be enjoyed chilled, warm, or even boiled as part of a Christmas Glühwein. It also works as a cocktail base, or paired with dark chocolate.
